Tophat & Spears

The swarm is coming over the ridge from the north. Six waves. Keep the relay standing. One player commands from above, up to four drop into soldiers on the ground. In this build you play both seats yourself and swap with Tab.

Tophat

  • Drag to select, 1 2 3 squads, Q all
  • Right-click to move, P then click to patrol
  • X hold position, WASD pan, wheel zoom
  • Ordering the Spear's squad starts their leash clock

Spear

  • WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, Shift sprint
  • Aim at a marine and press E to hop
  • Small buffs only: a bit more HP and damage
  • Miss the leash and you bleed. Die early and you wait out the hop cooldown

Built for keyboard and mouse. Open this on a laptop to play.
