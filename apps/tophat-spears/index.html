Tophat & Spears

The swarm is coming over the ridge from the north. Six waves. Keep the relay standing. One player commands from above as the Tophat. Up to four Spears drop into soldiers on the ground and hop between their bodies.

Tophat

  • Drag to select, 1 2 3 squads, Q all
  • Right-click to move, P then click to patrol
  • X hold position, WASD pan, wheel zoom
  • Ordering a Spear's body starts their leash clock

Spear

  • WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, Shift sprint
  • Aim at a marine and press E to hop
  • Small buffs only: a bit more HP and damage
  • Miss the leash and you bleed. Die early and you wait out the hop cooldown
