How to play
  • One face in every color. Each colored region gets exactly one.
  • One face in every row and column. A face claims its whole row and column.
  • No touching. Not side by side, not corner to corner.
  • No guessing. Every board can be worked out. A wrong tap costs a heart, and three wrong taps ends the round.
  • Switch to ✕ to mark cells you've ruled out. Drag to mark a bunch at once.
  • Stuck? Hint points at the next thing you can work out, and says why.
