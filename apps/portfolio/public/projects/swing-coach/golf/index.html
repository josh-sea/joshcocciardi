SwingGolf

Two tools. One reads your swing through the camera. The other walks the course with you.

🎥

Analyzer

Point a camera at your swing and it reads posture, coil, head movement, the kinematic sequence and your finish, then talks you through the fixes. Works live or from a clip.

Caddy

Mark where you are and where you're going on a satellite map, and it tells you the club and the target. Every shot you log teaches it how far you really hit each club.

Open caddy →

The analyzer runs a pose model on your device and never uploads video. The caddy uses your phone's GPS and keeps your bag and your rounds in your account, so they follow you between devices.
