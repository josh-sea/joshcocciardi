How the swing analyzer works

A pose model (Google's MediaPipe BlazePose) runs in the tab and finds 33 body landmarks per frame. Everything after that is plain geometry: joint angles, how far the head travels, how fast the hands move, and when the hips and shoulders each reach their peak turn. It never sees the bat, the club or the ball, and the thresholds are coaching rules of thumb rather than lab measurements.

Nothing is uploaded. The model runs on your device and your video never leaves it.
