Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026

Roy ("the App") is a color strategy game developed by Josh Cocciardi ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username (chosen by you)

Game Data

When you play, we store:

  • Game history and scores
  • In-game preferences and settings

Device Information

To deliver push notifications, we collect:

  • Push notification token (provided by Expo/your device)

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable multiplayer gameplay between you and other players
  • Send push notifications about game activity (your turn, game invites, in-game messages)
  • Save your game preferences and settings

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Expo Push Notifications (delivering notifications) — Expo Privacy Policy

5. Push Notifications

You can control which notifications you receive in the App's settings. You can also disable notifications entirely through your device's system settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to play the game.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Game data for completed games may be retained to display historical stats. If you delete your account, all of your data is permanently removed (see below).

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time by visiting:

https://battle-roy-al.web.app/delete-account.html

When you delete your account, we remove:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile, username, and preferences
  • Your game history
  • Your push notification token

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
