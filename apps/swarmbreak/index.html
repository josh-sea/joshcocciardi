Co-op alien sweep · 1 to 4 marines

SWARMBREAK

The station went quiet a week ago. Now it's crawling. Sweep it deck by deck: burn out every nest, grab whatever heavier guns the last crew dropped, and get the whole squad onto the exit pad. Six decks down, something is laying all those eggs.

One computer

Share the keyboard (two marines), add gamepads for three or four.

Online lobby

Everyone types the same password. Up to four marines across every computer.

Field guide

  • Crawlers fast, weak, many. Nests keep making them.
  • Brutes slow and armoured. Back up while you shoot.
  • Spitters keep their distance and lob acid.
  • Nests pulse in the dark. Every one must die before the exit unlocks.
  • 1 Pulse Carbine never runs dry.
  • 2 Scattergun a wall of pellets up close.
  • 3 Torch burns through a whole crowd.
  • 4 Lance one beam through everything in a line.
  • 5 Boomtube rockets. Mind the splash.

The squad shares six respawns a level. If they run out and everyone is down, you retry the deck.
